ArcFlow: plasma CAM that works like a synergic welder

Offline summary of the design for whoever builds it. Pixel truth is the live design at claude.ai/artifact/UfHut5G4ZC6xxEBJCpacM1. Tokens and rules here match tokens.css and docs/design/*.md.

Main flow

Drop a DXF, pick material, press Generuj. Every automatic decision has a reason and a one-click restore.

Upuść DXF→Raport importu→S235 · 4 mm · 45 A→Generuj→Ścieżki + Dlaczego?→Korekty→Symulacja→Walidacja→Zapisz G-code

Synergic

Everything automatic. You accept. Value = your override, otherwise auto.

Assisted

Automation proposes, you approve contour by contour. Problems panel with Zalecane options.

Manual

Automation off, like SheetCAM. Validation still warns and blocks errors.

Tool card

Title with shortcut (L Lead-in), one-line hint, fields, Esc Anuluj / OK ↵. Recomputes live while you drag the start point.

Auto vs manual

Grey value = automatic. White + blue dot = manual, with "Zmienione ręcznie z X · przywróć auto".

Impact line

After a change: "Wpływ: +4 pętle · +75 mm cięcia · +1,2 s" or "−2 przebicia · −1,8 s".

Tooltips

After 0,6 s (long-press on touch). Title, one sentence, 2–4 diagrams in CAM colours, footer with this project's value and its source.
